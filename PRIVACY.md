# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 23, 2026

## Overview

GitLab Issue/MR Number Pin ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the Extension handles data.

## Data Collection

**The Extension does not collect, transmit, or share any user data.**

All functionality operates entirely within your browser. The Extension:
- ✅ Does NOT collect personal information
- ✅ Does NOT track browsing history
- ✅ Does NOT send data to external servers
- ✅ Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
- ✅ Does NOT share data with third parties

## Local Storage

The Extension uses browser local storage to save your preferences, including:
- Custom badge styles (colors, fonts, sizes, positions)
- GitLab instance URLs for self-hosted installations
- Badge visibility settings
- Full-repository mode preferences

**All data is stored locally on your device and never leaves your browser.**

## Permissions

### Storage Permission
Required to save your configuration settings locally in the browser. No data is transmitted externally.

### Scripting Permission
Required to inject content scripts into GitLab pages to display issue and merge request number badges. Scripts only run on GitLab domains and do not access sensitive user data or credentials.

### Host Permissions (Optional)
The Extension requests optional host permissions for GitLab domains (both GitLab.com and self-hosted instances). These permissions are only used to:
- Detect issue and merge request numbers on GitLab pages
- Render badge overlays on relevant UI components

You control which domains the Extension can access through Chrome's extension settings.

## Third-Party Services

The Extension does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs.

## Data Security

Since the Extension does not collect or transmit data, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to user information. All settings remain on your local device.

## Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13. The Extension is not directed at children.
